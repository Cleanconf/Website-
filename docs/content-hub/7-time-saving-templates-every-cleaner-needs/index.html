7 Time-Saving Templates Every Cleaner Needs

Published 12 November 2026 · 2 min read · by the OSO Organised team

Writing the same kind of message over and over takes longer than it should, especially when you're trying to get the tone right. Here are seven templates to copy, paste and send as they are, or tweak to sound like you.

1. Confirming a new booking

Hi [name], thanks for booking in! I've got you down for [day], [date] at [time]. I'll confirm the day before. Looking forward to it!

2. Reminding a client the day before

Hi [name], just a quick reminder I'll be round tomorrow at [time] for your clean. Let me know if anything's changed.

3. Chasing a late payment, first message

Hi [name], hope you're well! Just a quick reminder that this week's clean (£[amount]) is still outstanding. Bank details are below if that's easiest. Thanks so much!

4. Following up on a late payment

Hi [name], following up on my message from [day]. The payment of £[amount] for [date]'s clean is now [number] days overdue. Could you let me know when I can expect it?

5. Asking to reschedule

Hi [name], something's come up and I need to move our clean on [date]. Would [new day/time] work instead? Sorry for the short notice.

6. A new client welcome

Hi [name], lovely to have you on board! A few quick things: I'll usually arrive within a 30-minute window of the agreed time, and payment is due on the day unless we've agreed otherwise. Any questions, just ask.

7. Asking for a repeat booking

Hi [name], hope you were happy with today's clean! If you'd like to book a regular slot, just let me know what day and time usually suits, and I'll get it set up.
