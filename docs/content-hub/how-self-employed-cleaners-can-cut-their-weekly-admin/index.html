How Self-Employed Cleaners Can Cut Their Weekly Admin

Published 5 November 2026 · 2 min read · by the OSO Organised team

Before you can cut your admin time, it helps to know where it's actually going. Try timing yourself for a few days: how long does booking a job take, chasing a payment, working out your week? Most people are surprised by the answer.

Admin time isn't just a cleaner's problem. UK businesses affected by late payment spend an average of 86 hours a year chasing it, around 1.7 hours a week, according to DBT and Small Business Commissioner research from July 2025. That figure covers all UK business types, not cleaners specifically, and only 22% of surveyed businesses reported it, so treat it as a sign of scale rather than your own number. The real way to know your own total is to time your own week.

Here's where OSO Organised takes pieces of that admin off your plate.

Clients

Every client has one profile: contact details, address, booking history and what they owe. No more searching old texts for an address you wrote down once.

A calendar that repeats itself

Set up a regular clean once and it repeats on your calendar automatically, weekly, fortnightly or monthly. OSO also warns you if two jobs are about to overlap, so you catch it before it becomes a problem.

A clear list of who's paid

Your payments list shows who's paid and who still owes you, at a glance. No more totting it up from memory or a notebook at the end of the week.

A reminder ready to send

When a payment is late, Hello Lina flags it for you and writes a reminder message. You check it and send it whenever you choose, it's never sent automatically without you.

One simple view of your business

The dashboard shows your income over time, how many hours you've worked this month, and how many clients you've got on the books. No spreadsheets required.

None of this is about adding more to your day. It's about not having to hold it all in your head, a notebook and a handful of apps at once.
