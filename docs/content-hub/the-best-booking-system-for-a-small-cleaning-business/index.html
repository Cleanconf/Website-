The Best Booking System for a Small Cleaning Business

Published 12 November 2026 · 1 min read · by the OSO Organised team

A booking system for a small cleaning business needs to do one thing well: hold every job with the details that matter, time, client, address, service and price, in one place you can trust.

Why a notebook or phone calendar falls short

A notebook holds the time and maybe a name, but not much else, and there's no backup if you lose it. A phone calendar is quick to check, but a bare time slot doesn't tell you the address, what the client pays, or whether this is a regular clean or a one-off. Both leave you flipping between a calendar, a notebook and your messages to piece a job together.

One glance and you know your day

OSO Organised keeps every job in one calendar, with the client, address, service and price attached to the booking itself. No flipping between apps to find the details.

Set a regular clean once

Set up a weekly, fortnightly or monthly clean once, and it repeats on your calendar automatically. You don't have to re-book it each time or trust yourself to remember.

A warning before you double-book

If two jobs are about to overlap, OSO warns you before it becomes a problem, not after you've turned up to two addresses at once.

What this actually saves you

Not time in the sense of doing more. Time in the sense of not having to hold your whole week in your head, a notebook and a phone calendar at once.
