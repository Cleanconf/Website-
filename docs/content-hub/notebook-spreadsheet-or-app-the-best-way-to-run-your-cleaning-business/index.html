Notebook, Spreadsheet or App: The Best Way to Run Your Cleaning Business

Published 5 November 2026 · 2 min read · by the OSO Organised team

Most cleaners don't use one tool to run their business. It's usually a mix: a notebook for bookings, texts for reminders, a WhatsApp thread for everything else. Here's how each one actually holds up, and what changes when it's all in one place.

The notebook

A notebook goes everywhere with you, and there's nothing to charge or log into. But there's no backup. If you lose it, or a page gets wet on a job, that information is gone. There's no quick way to search for a client's address from three months ago, and nothing warns you if you've double-booked two jobs for the same time.

The phone calendar

Your phone calendar is good for a quick glance at today. The trouble starts when you need more than a time slot: a client's address, what they pay, whether this is a one-off or a regular clean. None of that fits neatly into a calendar event, so it ends up scattered across notes, texts and memory instead.

WhatsApp and texts

Fast to send, and most clients reply quickly. But a reminder sent last month is buried under weeks of other messages by the time you need to find it again. There's no single view of who still owes you money, and nothing that pulls today's jobs together in one place.

A spreadsheet

A spreadsheet can hold a lot: names, prices, dates, notes. The problem is updating it on the move. Typing into small cells on your phone between jobs is slow, and it's easy to overwrite something by mistake or lose track of which version is the latest.

One simple app

OSO Organised puts your clients, bookings and payments in one place instead of four. Set up a regular clean once and it repeats on your calendar automatically, with a warning if two jobs ever overlap. Every client has one profile: contact details, address, booking history and what they owe. When a payment is late, Hello Lina flags it for you, with a reminder message ready to send whenever you choose.

Nothing here is about doing more. It's about not having to hold all of it in your head, a notebook and three different apps at once.

The short version

  • A notebook has no backup and nothing to search
  • A phone calendar can't hold client or payment details
  • WhatsApp and texts bury old reminders fast
  • A spreadsheet is awkward to update on the move
  • One simple app keeps clients, bookings and payments together, with regular cleans that repeat themselves and reminders ready to send when something's overdue
