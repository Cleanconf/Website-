Privacy Policy

Version 10/11/2026

1. Introduction

1.1 Purpose.

This Privacy Policy explains how OSO Organised ("OSO", "we", "us") collects, uses, shares and protects personal data in connection with the Platform and your Account.

1.2 Controller.

OSO Organised (operated by Lucy Toleman and Joanna Ansell) is the data controller for personal data collected for registration, billing and service provision. Contact: hello@osoorganised.co.uk.

2. Scope

2.1 Applicability.

This Privacy Policy applies to personal data of Users and to the processing of Client personal data that you input into the Platform ("Business Data"). Where OSO acts as a processor for Business Data, OSO processes that data on your instruction.

3. Categories of personal data

3.1 Data we collect as controller (User Account & billing):

  • Identity data (name, username)
  • Business name
  • Contact data (email, telephone, postal address)
  • Billing/payment data (payment card tokenised details via payment processor; billing address)
  • Account data (subscription plan, usage meta-data, support correspondence, and records of the Terms and Privacy Policy versions you accepted and of your analytics choices, with the date and time)
  • Technical data (IP address, device and browser information, access logs, login timestamps)

3.2 Data processed as processor (Business Data you upload):

  • Client contact details (name, phone, email, address)
  • Appointment and service records (dates, services provided, notes excluding health/special category data)
  • Communication logs between User and Client, if stored on the Platform

3.3 Special category data.

We do not request or store health or other special category data. You must not upload such data.

4. Purposes and lawful bases for processing

4.1 Purposes.

We process personal data for the following purposes:

  • (a) To provide, operate and maintain the Platform (performance of contract — Art. 6(1)(b) UK GDPR)
  • (b) To manage payments and billing (performance of contract)
  • (c) To communicate service messages, account notifications and support (performance of contract; legitimate interests — Art. 6(1)(f))
  • (d) To understand how the Platform is used and improve it, through product analytics and session recordings. We only do this if you agree when asked in the Platform, and you can withdraw your agreement at any time from your Profile (consent — Art. 6(1)(a)). See section 12 for details.
  • (e) To keep the Platform secure, prevent fraud and fix faults, using log and diagnostic data (legitimate interests — Art. 6(1)(f))
  • (f) To comply with legal obligations (legal obligation — Art. 6(1)(c))
  • (g) To keep a record of the Terms and Privacy Policy versions you accepted and of your analytics choices, so we can show what you agreed to (legitimate interests — Art. 6(1)(f); for analytics choices, legal obligation — Art. 6(1)(c))

4.2 Processing of Business Data.

For Client personal data you upload, OSO acts as processor and will process that data only on your documented instructions (Article 28 UK GDPR).

4.3 Marketing communications.

We may use your contact details (including email address and mobile number) to send you marketing communications about OSO Organised, including new features, new service tiers, product launches, offers, promotions, business updates, ownership updates, industry insights, tips, guides and other content relevant to your use of the Platform.

4.4 Third‑party promotions and sponsored content.

We may include third‑party product recommendations, sponsored content or advertising within our emails, articles, guides or other content we send to you. Such content is selected by OSO Organised; we do not share your personal data with advertisers unless you explicitly consent.

4.5 Legal basis for marketing.

Our legal basis for sending you marketing communications is either (a) your consent, or (b) the "soft opt‑in" under the Privacy and Electronic Communications Regulations (PECR), where you have subscribed to our Services and we are marketing similar products or services.

4.6 WhatsApp and SMS communications.

Where you provide a mobile number, you consent to us contacting you via SMS or WhatsApp for service and marketing communications. You may withdraw this consent at any time by replying STOP or adjusting your preferences in your account settings.

5. Consent and user responsibilities

5.1 Consent.

Where processing requires consent (e.g., promotional messages), we will obtain consent where required by law. You consent to service messages and transactional communications necessary for account and subscription administration.

5.2 Your obligations.

You must ensure you have a lawful basis to store and process Client personal data and obtain any consents required by applicable law (clause 5.3 of the Terms of Service). You must not upload special category data.

6. Sharing and disclosure

6.1 Service providers.

We share personal data with processors who perform services for OSO (payment processors, hosting providers, analytics providers). We require processors to implement appropriate safeguards.

6.2 Legal requirement.

We may disclose personal data to comply with legal obligations, to defend legal claims, or to respond to lawful requests by public authorities.

6.3 Business transfers.

On sale or reorganisation of OSO we may transfer personal data to the buyer as part of the business transfer, subject to confidentiality obligations.

7. International transfers

7.1 Transfers.

Personal data may be processed by or stored with service providers outside the UK/EEA. Where transfers occur OSO will ensure adequate safeguards (UK adequacy decision, Standard Contractual Clauses or other appropriate safeguards) are in place.

8. Data retention

8.1 Retention periods.

We retain personal data only as long as necessary for the purposes set out:

  • (a) Account and billing data: for the duration of the subscription and for a period of up to 7 years thereafter for tax, accounting and legal compliance unless a different period is required by law
  • (b) Business Data: for the duration you maintain the Account and for the period set out in clause 14 of the Terms after termination unless you export data earlier
  • (c) Log and diagnostic data: for up to 2 years for security, fraud prevention and analytics
  • (d) Anonymous data may be retained outside of the above for OSO Organised legitimate business purposes (e.g. insight and analysis)
  • (e) Records of the documents you accepted and of your analytics choices: for the duration of the subscription and for up to 7 years thereafter, so we can show what you agreed to. These records do not include your name or contact details

8.2 Deletion.

On deletion of your Account OSO will delete or anonymise personal data within 30 days except where retention is required or permitted by law.

9. Security

9.1 Measures.

We implement technical and organisational measures to protect personal data including encryption in transit, secure hosting, access controls and logging.

9.2 No absolute security.

Whilst we endeavour to protect personal data, no system is completely secure. Report any suspected breach to hello@osoorganised.co.uk immediately.

10. Your rights

10.1 Rights available.

Subject to applicable law, you may have rights including:

  • (a) access to your personal data;
  • (b) rectification of inaccurate data;
  • (c) erasure (right to be forgotten) in limited circumstances;
  • (d) restriction of processing;
  • (e) data portability;
  • (f) objection to processing based on legitimate interests;
  • (g) withdraw consent where processing is based on consent.

10.2 Exercising rights.

To exercise rights contact hello@osoorganised.co.uk. We will verify requests and respond within one month (extensions may apply).

10.3 Complaints.

You have the right to lodge a complaint with the UK Information Commissioner's Office (ICO) at ico.org.uk if you are unhappy with our handling of your data.

11. Automated decision‑making

11.1 No significant automated decisions.

We do not use automated decision-making that produces legal or similarly significant effects for Users. We may use automated processing (e.g., analytics) for legitimate business purposes.

12. Cookies and similar technologies

"Cookies" here includes cookies and similar technologies, such as your browser's local storage, which save small pieces of information on your device. We keep their use to a minimum.

When you first use the Platform, we ask whether you accept analytics. You can change your choice at any time under Profile → Enable analytics / Withdraw consent. If you withdraw, we stop collecting analytics data straight away.

Our website and Platform load fonts from Google Fonts, which means your IP address is shared with Google. Google Fonts does not set cookies.

13. Children

13.1 Age limit.

The Platform is intended for business users. We do not knowingly collect personal data from children under 13. If you believe we have collected such data, contact us to request deletion.

14. Changes to this policy

14.1 Updates.

We may update this Privacy Policy. Material changes will be notified via the Platform or email. Continued use after notification constitutes acceptance.

15. Contact and Data Protection Officer

15.1 Contact.

For privacy enquiries contact: hello@osoorganised.co.uk.

15.2 DPO.

No DPO appointed; contact as above.
