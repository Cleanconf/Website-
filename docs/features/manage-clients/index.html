Manage clients: the simple client management app for cleaners

OSO Organised is a client management app for cleaners who work on their own. Every client gets one tidy profile with their details, bookings, payments and what they still owe.

Common questions

How do I keep track of my cleaning clients?

Add each client to OSO once. Their profile keeps their contact details, address, bookings, payments and anything they owe, all in one place.

Can I see how much a client has paid me?

Yes. Each profile shows the total that client has brought in and how many jobs you've done for them.

Can I set up recurring bookings?

Yes. Add a regular clean once, weekly, fortnightly or monthly, and OSO keeps it in your calendar so you never have to retype it.

Does it work on my phone?

Yes. OSO is built for your phone first, so you can check a client's details between jobs.

What happens if a client cancels?

Cancel the booking and it comes off your calendar. Your payments list updates too, so it stays accurate.
