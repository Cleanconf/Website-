The problem

A regular clean, a deep clean, an end-of-tenancy clean: they all take different amounts of time and deserve different prices. When your prices live in your head, it's easy to undercharge, forget what you quoted or charge two clients differently for the same job.

How OSO solves it

Build your services list for bespoke pricing you can track.

Your cleaning services price list, in one place

List every clean you offer with a name, how long it takes and its price. Give each one a colour, and it shows on your calendar too.

Every booking priced right

Common questions

How should I price different cleaning jobs?

Price each type of clean by how long it takes and how much work is involved. A deep clean usually takes longer than a regular clean, so it should cost more. In OSO, you can set a separate time and price for each service.

What can I add to a service?

A name, how long it takes, its price and a colour.

How do services link to my bookings?

When you add a booking, you pick the service and it's linked to that job, so you can see what you're doing and what you're charging.

If I change a price, do my existing bookings update?

No. Bookings you've already made keep their original price.
