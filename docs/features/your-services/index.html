Common questions

Can I set a different price for each service?

Yes. Give each service its own name, price and duration.

Do I need to pick a service every time I add a booking?

Yes — pick the service and its price and duration come with it automatically.

If I change a price, does it update old bookings?

It updates new bookings going forward. Bookings already on your calendar keep the price they were set up with.

Can I tell services apart at a glance?

Yes — give each one a colour so you can spot it on your calendar in a second.
