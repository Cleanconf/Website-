The problem

Your client list lives in your head, your phone contacts and a notebook in the car. Addresses, key codes and "she likes the kitchen done last" are all in different places. Sooner or later, two jobs clash or a visit gets missed.

How OSO solves it

Everything about a client and every booking in one place, on your phone.

One client, one card

Name, address, contact details and notes — all together. Add key-safe codes, parking tips, allergies and preferences so you're never guessing on the doorstep.

A calendar that keeps you straight

See today and the week ahead at a glance. Set a regular clean once and OSO repeats it for you. If two jobs would overlap, you'll see it before it becomes a problem.

Common questions

Can I set up recurring bookings?

Yes. Add a regular clean once — weekly, fortnightly or monthly — and OSO keeps it in your calendar so you never have to retype it.

Can I add notes about a property?

Yes. Each client has space for notes like key-safe codes, parking, pets and cleaning preferences, so the details are always to hand.

Does it work on my phone?

Yes. OSO is built for your phone first, so you can check your day and update bookings between jobs.

What happens if a client cancels?

Mark the booking as cancelled in a tap. It comes off your calendar and your payment list stays accurate.
