Common questions

Do I need to be good with numbers?

No. The dashboard is designed in plain language with simple charts. If you can read your bank balance, you can read this.

What does the dashboard show?

Your income over time, how many bookings you've done, your regular clients, and which days and weeks are busiest or quietest.

Can I export my data?

Yes. You can export your information to share with an accountant or keep your own records.

Where do the numbers come from?

From the bookings and payments you already record in OSO. Nothing extra to enter — the dashboard updates itself.
