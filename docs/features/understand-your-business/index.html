Understand your business: a simple income tracker for solo cleaners

OSO Organised is an income tracker for solo cleaners. Your bookings and payments turn into a simple dashboard that shows how your business is really doing, in numbers anyone can read.

Try OSO Free

The problem

You're working hard and the diary looks full, but is the business actually growing? Most sole traders can't say for sure. There's no time to build spreadsheets, and when your tax return comes round, you're piecing it all together from bank statements.

How OSO solves it

Your bookings and payments become a picture of your business, automatically.

An income tracker that fills itself in

Every payment you mark in OSO feeds a simple month-by-month chart of your income. Spot quieter months early and plan around the busy ones.

Your month in plain numbers

See the hours you've worked this month and how many active clients you have, next to your income and what you're owed this month.

The benefits

Confident decisions

Thinking about putting your prices up or booking a holiday? Check what the numbers say first.

Spot quieter months early

See when your income dips, so you can plan ahead instead of worrying.

No spreadsheets

The dashboard builds itself from what you already record.

Ready for your tax return

Know what you've earned at any point, so you can plan how much to keep back for tax.

Common questions

How do I know what my cleaning business earns?

Mark your jobs as paid in OSO and your dashboard shows your income month by month, so you always know where you stand.

Do I need to be good with numbers?

No. The dashboard uses plain language and a simple chart. If you can read your bank balance, you can read this.

What does the dashboard show?

Your income month by month, the hours you've worked this month and how many active clients you have this month.

Where do the numbers come from?

From the bookings and payments you already record in OSO. There's nothing extra to enter.
