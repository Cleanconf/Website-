Every job tracked, every total added up, and the words written for you.

Common questions

Does OSO take card payments?

Not yet. For now OSO helps you track what you're owed and send reminders. Taking payments in-app is on our roadmap — Coming soon

Can I see what I'm owed this month?

Yes. OSO adds up your unpaid jobs so you always know the total, and shows your income once payments come in.

How do payment reminders work?

OSO gives you a friendly, ready-written message you can send to a client whose payment is overdue, so you don't have to find the words.

Is my data safe?

Yes. Your client and payment information is stored securely and is only visible to you.
