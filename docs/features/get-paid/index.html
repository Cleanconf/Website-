Get paid: a simple payment tracker for cleaners

OSO Organised includes a simple payment tracker for cleaners. Mark each job paid or unpaid, see what you're owed at a glance, and get a polite message to send when someone's late.

Try OSO Free

The problem

Chasing money is the job nobody enjoys. It's easy to lose track of who's paid, awkward to bring up, and stressful when the numbers don't add up at the end of the month. So it gets put off, and late payments pile up.

How OSO solves it

Every job tracked, every total added up, and the words written for you.

Paid or unpaid, always clear

Mark a job paid when the money lands. OSO keeps a running list of what's still outstanding, so you're never guessing.

A payment tracker that does the adding up

See your income and the total you're still owed, overall, for each client or for any period. No calculator, no notebook and no surprises when your tax return is due.

Friendly reminders, ready to send

When a payment is late, OSO gives you a polite, ready-written message. Check it, copy it into your own text, email or WhatsApp, and send. You stay on good terms without the awkward chat.

The benefits

No awkward chats

The message is already written. You just check it and send.

Know your numbers

Your income and what you're owed, clear at any point in the month.

Nothing slips through

Every job you mark as done lands on your payments list, so no clean goes unpaid by accident.

Less month-end stress

Everything's recorded as you go, so there's nothing to piece together later.

Common questions

How do I keep track of which clients have paid?

Mark each job paid or unpaid in OSO. It keeps a running total of what you're owed, overall and for each client.

Can I see what I'm owed this month?

Yes. OSO adds up your unpaid jobs so you always know the total, and shows your income as payments come in.

How do payment reminders work?

OSO gives you a polite, ready-written message for any late payment. You check it, copy it into your own text, email or WhatsApp, and send it yourself. Nothing is sent automatically.
