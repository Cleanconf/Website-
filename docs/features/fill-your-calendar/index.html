Fill your calendar: the simple cleaning booking app

OSO Organised is a cleaning booking app for cleaners who work on their own. See each day's jobs with the time, client, address, service and price, all in one place.

Try OSO Free

The problem

Your bookings are in a paper diary, your phone calendar and a string of WhatsApp messages. You're rewriting the same fortnightly cleans every month, and it only takes one slip for two jobs to land at the same time.

How OSO solves it

Every job in one calendar you can trust, with everything you need for the day.

A cleaning booking app that shows your whole day

Open OSO and see today's jobs in order, with the time, client, address, service and price. Adding a new job is quick, and the client and service link straight to it.

Set regular cleans once

Weekly, fortnightly or monthly: set a regular clean up once and OSO keeps it in your calendar. If two jobs would overlap, OSO warns you first.

Done means done

Mark a job as done when you finish and it moves to your payments list, ready to track. Cancel a booking and it comes off your calendar, and your payments list updates too.

The benefits

No more double bookings

OSO warns you before two jobs overlap, so clashes are easy to avoid.

Nothing forgotten

Every visit is in your calendar, including the fortnightly ones that are easy to lose track of.

Set it once

Recurring bookings save you re-entering the same jobs week after week.

Everything for the day

Time, address, service and price for every job, so you're not checking three places before you leave the house.

Common questions

How do I stop double booking my cleaning clients?

Book every job in OSO. If a new booking would overlap with another one, OSO warns you.

Can I set up fortnightly cleans?

Yes. Set a regular clean to repeat weekly, fortnightly or monthly and OSO keeps it in your calendar.

What happens when I mark a job as done?

It moves to your payments list, so you can track whether you've been paid.

What happens if a client cancels?

Cancel the booking and it comes off your calendar. Your payments list updates too.
