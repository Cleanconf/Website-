OSO features

Manage clients

One tidy profile for every client, with contact details, address, booking and payment history, and what they still owe. See how much each client has brought in and how many jobs you've done for them.

Explore this feature

Fill your calendar

A clear daily view showing the time, client, address, service and price for every job. Set regular cleans once, get a warning before jobs overlap, and mark each one done when you finish.

Explore this feature

Get paid

Mark jobs paid or unpaid, and see a running total of what you've earned and what you're owed: overall, for each client or for any period.

Explore this feature

Understand your business

A plain-language dashboard built from your bookings and payments. See your income over time, your hours this month and your active clients.

Explore this feature

Hello Lina

Lina sits in the corner of every page. Tap her to see who hasn't paid, and get a polite, ready-written reminder to copy into your own text, email or WhatsApp.

Your services

List every clean you offer, with its name, time, price and colour. Pick a service when you book and the details come with it.
