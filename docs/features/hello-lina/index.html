Common questions

Who is Lina?

Lina is the friendly helper built into OSO Organised. She helps you chase late payments by showing who hasn't paid and giving you a ready-written reminder.

How do I ask a client to pay without it being awkward?

Keep it short, friendly and clear. Lina gives you a polite, ready-written reminder, so you don't have to find the words yourself.

Does Lina send messages for me?

No. You check the message, then copy it into your own text, email or WhatsApp and send it yourself.

Where do I find Lina?

In the corner of every page in OSO. Tap her to see who hasn't paid.

What else can Lina help with?

Right now, Lina focuses on late payments, the job most cleaners dread. We're always working on new ways for her to help, shaped by what our users tell us.
