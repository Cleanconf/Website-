We're building this for you

OSO Organised is made by two working mums who watched people they love run brilliant little businesses with tools that kept getting in the way.

Our story

Hi — we're Lucy and Joanna. We both work, we both have kids, and between us we've spent years watching sole traders and microbusiness owners do the hard bit brilliantly and the admin bit the hard way.

The people we had in mind when we started are often women, self-employed, working in other people's homes, and holding the whole business in their head and their phone. A notebook for bookings. Texts for reminders. A separate app for invoices that never quite fit. Sticky notes for the rest.

None of those tools were built for this kind of work, and none of them talk to each other. So things get missed, payments run late, and Sunday night disappears into catching up.

We thought there should be one simple app that does the lot — clients, bookings, payments, reminders and a clear view of how the business is doing — without the jargon and without the faff. That's OSO Organised.

We're starting with cleaners because that's the world we know best, and we're building it in the open with the people who use it. If that's you, we'd love your input. 💜
