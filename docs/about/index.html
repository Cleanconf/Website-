A cleaning app made by two working mums, for you

OSO Organised is a cleaning app made by two working mums, for cleaners who run their own business. We built it because we watched people we love run brilliant little businesses with tools that kept getting in the way.

Our story

Hi, we're Lucy and Joanna. We met in the school playground and we're both working mums. We grew up in families of small business owners, so we know what it's like to juggle work, clients, kids and home all at once.

The people we had in mind are often women, self-employed and working in other people's homes, holding the whole business in their head and their phone. A notebook for bookings. Texts for reminders. Sticky notes for the rest.

None of those tools were built for this kind of work, and none of them talk to each other. So things get missed, payments run late, and Sunday night disappears into catching up.

We've built technology before, so we knew there was a better way: one simple app for your clients, bookings, payments and a clear view of how your business is doing. No jargon, no fuss. That's OSO Organised.

We started with cleaners because that's the world we know best. You're not "just a cleaner". You're a businesswoman, and you deserve tools that work as hard as you do. Because when small businesses thrive, communities thrive. 💜
