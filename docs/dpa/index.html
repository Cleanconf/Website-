Data Processing Agreement (DPA)

Version 12/11/2026

Between: OSO Organised ("Processor", "OSO"), operated by Lucy Toleman and Joanna Ansell, and the User ("Controller", "you").

Effective date: the date you subscribe to the Platform.

1. Definitions

  • "Business Data" means personal data relating to your Clients that you upload to or generate within the Platform.
  • "Data Protection Laws" means UK GDPR, the Data Protection Act 2018, PECR, and any applicable UK legislation.
  • "Services" means the OSO Organised SaaS platform.

2. Roles of the parties

  • 2.1 You are the Controller of Business Data.
  • 2.2 OSO is the Processor of Business Data.
  • 2.3 For User Account data, OSO is the Controller (see Privacy Policy).

3. Subject matter, duration, nature and purpose

  • 3.1 Subject matter: Processing of Business Data for provision of the Platform.
  • 3.2 Duration: For the duration of your subscription and any retention period described in the Privacy Policy.
  • 3.3 Nature: Storage, hosting, transmission, analytics, communication tools, scheduling.
  • 3.4 Purpose: To provide the Services and associated support.

4. Types of data and data subjects

4.1 Types of personal data:

  • Client names, contact details, appointment information, service notes (excluding special category data).
  • Communications between you and Clients (if stored).

4.2 Data subjects:

  • Your Clients
  • Your authorised users (if applicable)

5. Processor obligations

OSO shall:

  • 5.1 Process Business Data only on your documented instructions, except where required by law.
  • 5.2 Ensure personnel are subject to confidentiality obligations.
  • 5.3 Implement appropriate technical and organisational security measures.
  • 5.4 Assist you with data subject rights requests where feasible.
  • 5.5 Assist with DPIAs where required.
  • 5.6 Notify you without undue delay of any personal data breach.
  • 5.7 Delete or return Business Data on termination, subject to legal retention requirements.

6. Sub‑processors

  • 6.1 You authorise OSO to use sub‑processors necessary for the Services (e.g., hosting, analytics, payment processors).
  • 6.2 OSO will ensure sub‑processors are bound by equivalent data protection obligations.
  • 6.3 OSO will maintain a list of sub‑processors (below) and notify you of material changes.

7. International transfers

  • 7.1 Transfers outside the UK/EEA will be subject to appropriate safeguards (e.g., UK IDTA, SCCs).
  • 7.2 OSO will ensure equivalent protection for transferred data.

8. Audit rights

  • 8.1 You may request information necessary to demonstrate OSO's compliance.
  • 8.2 Formal audits may be conducted no more than once per year, on reasonable notice, and limited to what is necessary.

9. Liability

  • 9.1 Liability is governed by the limitation clauses in the Terms of Service.
  • 9.2 Nothing limits liability for non‑excludable legal obligations.

10. Termination

  • 10.1 This DPA terminates automatically when your subscription ends.
  • 10.2 OSO will delete or anonymise Business Data in accordance with the Privacy Policy.

Use of the Platform constitutes acceptance of this DPA.
