How OSO works

Four small steps. No spreadsheets, no training day.

1

Add your clients

Add each client once, with their name, address and contact details. Their bookings and payments build up on their profile as you go.

2

Fill your calendar

Book a job in seconds, and set regular cleans to repeat weekly, fortnightly or monthly. OSO warns you if two jobs overlap.

3

Track who's paid

Mark a job as done and it moves to your payments list. When a client is late, Lina gives you a polite message to send.

4

See how you're doing

See your income over time, your hours this month and your active clients, in plain numbers.
